Cut the Nets — Privacy Policy

Effective: August 30, 2026

The short version

Cut the Nets does not require an account and does not collect, store, or sell your personal information. Your tournaments, settings, and streaks are stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Saved tournaments, rosters, records, sound preferences, and onboarding state are kept in local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. None of this is transmitted to us.

Advertising

The iOS app may show ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Where required, the app respects your device's tracking preferences (App Tracking Transparency). Purchasing the ad-free upgrade removes third-party ad SDK requests entirely.

What we never collect

No name, email, contacts, location, photos, health data, or messages. No analytics profiles. No selling of any data to anyone.

Content

The app is a statistical simulation built on publicly available historical sports information. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any player, university, or athletic association. Players who wish to have their name removed may contact us and will be honored promptly.
