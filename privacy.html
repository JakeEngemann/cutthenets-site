Cut the Nets — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 4, 2026

The short version

Cut the Nets does not require an account, and we do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. Your tournaments, settings, and streaks are stored only on your device. The one exception is advertising: if you see ads, Google collects some data to serve them, and that is described in full below.

Data stored on your device

Saved tournaments, rosters, records, sound preferences, and onboarding state are kept in local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. None of this is transmitted to us, and there is no account or server that holds it.

Advertising

The iOS app may show ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Identifiers — a device identifier, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking.
  • Usage data — advertising data and interactions with the ads shown, such as whether an ad was viewed or tapped.
  • Diagnostics — crash and performance data relating to the ads SDK itself.

This is collected by Google, not by us, and is described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive it, and it is not linked to your identity, because the app has no account and never asks who you are.

The app shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking identifier is used, and respects your answer. Where required — including the EEA and the UK — the app also shows Google's consent form before requesting personalised ads. Purchasing the Remove Ads upgrade stops the ad SDK from being loaded at all, so none of the above is collected.

What we never collect

No name, email address, contacts, location, photos, health data, or messages. No analytics or crash-reporting service of our own — the app contains no analytics SDK. No profile of you, and no selling or sharing of any data with anyone for any purpose other than serving the ads described above.

Children

Cut the Nets is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can decline tracking at the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can remove ads entirely with the Remove Ads purchase. Deleting the app removes everything the app has stored on your device.

Content

The app is a statistical simulation built on publicly available historical sports information. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any player, university, or athletic association. Players who wish to have their name removed may contact us and will be honored promptly.
